\# 🛡️ SURAKSHA-XR



\### AR-Based Vocational Training Simulator for Industrial Safety



SURAKSHA-XR is an AR-based vocational training and assessment platform

designed to help industrial workers safely practice emergency response

procedures in simulated mining and manufacturing environments.



\## 🎯 Problem



Many industrial workers receive safety training through lectures,

manuals and videos but have limited opportunities to practically

experience emergency situations.



\## 💡 Solution



SURAKSHA-XR uses Augmented Reality (AR) simulations to recreate

critical workplace emergency scenarios such as fire and gas leaks.



Workers can:



\- Practice emergency response procedures

\- Make decisions in simulated situations

\- Receive instant feedback

\- Complete competency assessments

\- Identify their weaknesses

\- Receive training certificates



Administrators can:



\- Monitor worker training

\- Track pass/fail rates

\- Analyze competency performance

\- Identify common weaknesses

\- Verify certificates



\## 🧩 Core Components



\- 📱 Android AR Training Application

\- 🌐 Web-based Admin Dashboard

\- ⚙️ Node.js + Express Backend

\- 🗄️ MongoDB Database

\- 🤖 AI-based Competency Analysis

\- 🎫 Digital Certificate \& QR Verification
